How to use Little Days

한국어 · English · 日本語

One photo a day is all it takes. Every month, your days turn into a little book and a tape.

  1. Record today
  2. Several photos and captions
  3. Leave a smile on missed days
  4. Make stickers
  5. Put stickers in a book
  6. Books and tapes of special days
  7. You This Month: video and letter
  8. Save a book as PDF
  9. Home Screen widget

1. Record today

  1. On the Home calendar, tap today's yellow + box. Empty boxes from earlier days work the same way.
  2. Photos taken that day show up first. Pick the one you like and tap Next.
  3. Adjust the crop and tap Next to open the day.
  4. Write a line about today. On busy days, tap Just a photo is fine too and keep only the photo.

You can write later. Days with a photo but no words get a small yellow dot on the calendar.

2. Several photos and captions

  1. Pick several photos at once and the Edit Photos screen opens.
  2. The first photo is the main photo of the day (★ Main). Press and hold a photo, then drag to reorder.
  3. To take one out, tap it first, then tap the ✕ on its corner.
  4. To add more later, tap + next to the photo on the day screen.

A caption for each photo

  1. On Home, tap a recorded day to open its card.
  2. Tap … at the top right of a photo → Add Caption, and write up to 20 characters.

Captions show up in the book and tape too. Inside a book, use … → Edit note on a photo page.

3. Leave a smile on missed days

It's fine to miss a day. Instead of leaving it blank, you can mark it as a day that just went by.

  1. On the Home calendar, press and hold an empty + box.
  2. It turns into a smiling face.

Tap the smile once to clear it again. Then tap the + box to record that day. Future days and days before birth can't be marked.

4. Make stickers

  1. Tap the smiley button at the top left of Home (Stickers).
  2. Make a pack with Make a Sticker Pack, or the + at the bottom right if you already have one. Choose a color and a label.
  3. Tap Add Sticker under the pack, then take a photo or choose one.
  4. Your baby or a toy is cut out automatically. If there are several, tap just the ones you want and tap Save.

Opening a pack

Slide the scissors all the way along the dotted line to open the pack. On the opened sheet, drag stickers around and pinch to resize them. Tap a sticker to share it, add a note, or delete it.

A pack holds up to 8 stickers. From the pack's … menu you can share it as an image, closed or opened.

5. Put stickers in a book

  1. Open a book from the Library and turn to the page you want to decorate.
  2. Tap the smiley button at the bottom right (Place sticker).
  3. Tap a sticker in the drawer below to put it on the page.
  4. Tap a placed sticker to select it. Drag to move it, and use the corner handle to resize and rotate.
  5. Tap Done when you're finished.

To take a sticker off the page, select it and tap the red ✕. You can also make one on the spot with New Sticker in the drawer.

6. Books and tapes of special days

A book and a tape are made for every month on their own. For a first birthday or a trip, make a special edition with just those days.

  1. On Home, tap Library at the top left → + at the top right.
  2. Choose Range for start and end dates, or One Day for a single day.
  3. Enter a title and tap As a Book or As a Tape.

Special editions live in the Specials tab of the Library. Press and hold to reorder them, and change their dates later from … → Change dates. A one-day book includes every photo from that day.

7. You This Month: video and letter

  1. Below the Home calendar, tap Look Back on You This Month.
  2. The month's photos play like an old video tape. Save it to Photos or Share it.
  3. Write a letter to your baby on the postcard and tap Save. Tap the stamp to change its photo.

The letter also becomes a page in that month's book.

8. Save a book as PDF

  1. Open a book from the Library and tap … at the top right.
  2. Tap Export PDF. Three PDFs are made: combined, inner pages and cover. Save them to Files or share them.

The same menu lets you change the cover photo, cover crop and book title. If you're printing it, check Print Specs (trim size 94 × 128 mm).

9. Home Screen widget

Touch and hold your iPhone Home Screen → Edit → Add Widget → choose Little Days. The small widget shows a recent photo. The medium one shows your baby's age and the four latest photos. Tap the widget to go straight to today.
