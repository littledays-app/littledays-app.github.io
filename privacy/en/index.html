Little Days Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026

Little Days stores your baby's photos and memories only on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. Nothing is sent to the developer's servers.

1. Information the app handles

The app only handles what you enter or choose. There is no sign-up, and no email address or phone number is collected.

ItemStored inUsed for
Child's name (nickname) and birth dateDevice, iCloudShowing age in months and day count, book titles
Photos you choose and the time they were takenDevice, iCloudDaily entries, making books and videos
Notes, likes, and monthly lettersDevice, iCloudYour records and archive
App settings (colors, reminder time, etc.)DeviceRunning the app

The app does not copy your whole photo library. Only the photos you choose are saved in the app. Photos are saved as originals, so if an original contains location data, it may remain on your device and in your backup. The app does not use this location data.

2. Storage and sharing

Your records stay on your device and in your own iCloud. The developer cannot see them.

  • Device: Photos and records are stored on your iPhone.
  • Automatic backup: When your records change, they are backed up to the app's folder in your iCloud Drive. You can turn this off in Settings.
  • Exporting: PDFs, videos, and photos go only to the apps or people you choose.

Information stored in iCloud is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

3. Device permissions

All permissions are optional. You can use the rest of the app without them and change them anytime in iPhone Settings.

PermissionUsed when
PhotosShowing the photos taken on a given day, saving videos you make
CameraTaking a photo to make a sticker
Microphone, Speech RecognitionWriting notes by voice. Processed on the device when possible (some devices use Apple's servers). Recordings are not saved.
NotificationsThe evening reminder

4. Third parties, ads, and analytics

We do not sell or share personal information. There are no ads, tracking, or analytics tools.

The app only connects to Apple services (iCloud, speech recognition) and this privacy policy page. Crash reports are shared anonymously only if you allow sharing in iPhone Settings.

5. Retention and deletion

Your records are kept until you delete them, and you can delete them at any time.

  • Entries, photos, and letters can be deleted from the … menu on each screen.
  • Settings → Delete All Memories removes everything at once.
  • Data and backups in iCloud can be deleted in iPhone Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage.

6. Children's information

This app is used by parents and guardians. A child's name, photos, and records are entered by the parent or guardian and stored only on their device and in their iCloud.
